# Quality gates

## Engineering

- The title states the system and the question.
- The scope boundary is visible.
- Every important edge has a direction and a verb.
- Error, retry, async, and trust boundaries are explicit when relevant.
- Facts, assumptions, and recommendations use distinct semantics.
- A reader can trace the primary path in five to nine steps.

## Visual

- Text remains readable at 100% zoom.
- Labels do not overlap connectors or controls.
- Color is never the only carrier of meaning.
- Contrast meets WCAG AA for normal text.
- Keyboard focus is visible.
- Reduced-motion preference disables nonessential animation.
- The diagram fits 360 px and 1440 px viewports without horizontal page scroll.

## Delivery

- Source JSON validates.
- The HTML artifact is standalone.
- Browser console has no errors.
- Copy, theme, mode, and step controls work.
- The output contains no secrets or local absolute paths.
